Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
This is a general-purpose template, not legal advice, and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Data protection laws vary by country — have this reviewed before relying on it commercially, especially since your customers may span multiple countries.

1. What We Collect

When you use Company Tracker, we store:

  • Company account details (name, dashboard login, currency, plan)
  • Employee login details you create (username, hashed password, full name if provided)
  • Transaction records (amounts, categories, notes, receipt photos if attached)
  • Inventory and fixed asset records
  • Technical data such as IP addresses in server logs, for security purposes

2. How We Use It

Data is used solely to provide the Service to your company — storing your records, displaying your dashboard, and enabling your employees to log entries. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on servers operated by our infrastructure provider (Railway) and its underlying cloud hosting. Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing (bcrypt), never in plain text.

4. Data Isolation

Each company's data is kept separate from every other company using the Service. Your employees and dashboard login can only access your own company's records.

5. Receipts and Photos

Receipt images you attach to transactions are stored as part of your transaction records and are visible only to your company's dashboard login.

6. Your Rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account at any time by contacting us. Deleting your account will remove your company's data from active use.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward in backups, unless you request earlier deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

This Service is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
